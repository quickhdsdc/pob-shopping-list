Why a picker and not a number

The value on these mods is baked into the option, not into a range. “12% increased Axe Damage” is option 49; there is no threshold to relax.

Why “at least 1” matters

Demanding every enchant usually returns nothing. Loosening to one of the useful ones is what makes a cluster jewel findable at all.
